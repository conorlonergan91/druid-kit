# Routing rules (distilled from RestedXP's free Forever routes, 17 Nov)

Source studied: the public RXPGuides repo (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), Forever Alliance 11-20 route. These are the
*decisions* behind it, in our own words. Never copy their route text or step lists into our guide; use the rules.

## How they choose where to go
1. **Follow quest density, not zone level ranges.** A hub is worked until its quests run thin, then left in one
   planned trip. Night Elves stay in Darkshore from 11 to about 19 and never visit Westfall at all.
2. **One trip, many jobs.** A long trip is built to also pick up flight paths, train, shop, hand in class quests
   and accept the next hub's quests. The 19-20 Redridge block is reached through Stormwind: train, take the
   Deadmines quests, then Lakeshire.
3. **Hearth = free teleport.** Set the hearthstone in the hub you will come back to (Auberdine, later Lakeshire).
   Hearth only after the far end of a cluster; check the cooldown first.
   It is only free if it was set: the setting is its own step, done before you leave the hub.
3b. **Flight paths are collected on foot.** Each first visit to a flight master is a step of its own; a route that
   flies somewhere must have walked there once before.
4. **Batch at hubs.** Accept everything a hub offers in one visit and hand everything in in one visit, so
   each NPC is visited as few times as possible.

## How they choose what to do
5. **Overlap objectives.** While travelling to quest B, kill or loot for quests A and C in the same area
   ("complete with"). Collect quest items you'll need later before you even have the quest.
6. **Cut low-value quests.** Long walks for small XP, solo elites, turn-ins off the route and dungeon quests
   without a group are abandoned, not done for completeness (they drop For Love Eternal, the druid Cure
   Poison chain and the Deadmines quests when no dungeon is planned).
7. **Level gates, small explicit grinds.** Steps are skipped when you're too low or already past them. Gaps are
   filled with a short grind to an exact target ("18 and 75%") at the densest mob spot next to the next
   hand-in, never with low-XP quests.
8. **Buy to save time.** Quest items and cheap upgrades come from the vendor or Auction House when that is
   faster than farming, after a money check.
9. **Class detours only when they pay.** Druid class quests ride on trips already planned (Teleport: Moonglade
   plus the Darnassus flight); training happens whenever the route passes a trainer, never as its own trip.
10. **Travel tricks are optional extras.** Dying near a graveyard on purpose ("death skip") and logout skips
    save minutes; we only mention them as optional, never as required.

## How they write a step
11. **One interaction per step**, naming the NPC, with coordinates for every place, and a short warning only
    where something is dangerous (leashed pairs, mobs that flee, knock-backs off ledges).
12. **Rewards are chosen per class** in the step itself, with a reason when it's not obvious.
13. **Every step can check itself:** quest accepted, completed or handed in, the zone reached, the level reached,
    an item count, a flight taken. Only true manual steps need a click.

## Applying it to a route
- Prefer dense hubs (one town, many quests, a hearth) over spreading across several low-density zones.
- When a trip goes to a hub anyway, do that hub's quests on the same visit, with the hearth set there.
- Dungeon quests stay only while a group is likely; otherwise abandon them.
- Low-value leftovers from earlier zones get cut.
- Write explicit "grind to X% at <spot>" steps instead of vague "grind here if short".
